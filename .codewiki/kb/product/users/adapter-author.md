# Adapter Author

Adapter authors implement user-facing clients or Runtime-selected execution harnesses. They need stable contracts for bounded reads, authenticated requests, Candidate production, Model Checks, workers, cancellation, usage reporting, and isolation.

Success means a new adapter can participate without duplicating a Loop, scheduler, canonical store, policy engine, or provider authority.
